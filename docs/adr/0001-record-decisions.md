# 0001. Record architecture decisions

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-11

## Context

The app's design history was kept as a long diary. New team members need the handful of
decisions that constrain their work without reading all of it.

## Decision

Keep short ADRs in `docs/adr/`, one per decision, numbered. The ADRs are the record of the
project's design decisions; each says what was decided, why, and what follows from it.

## Consequences

A change that reverses or extends one of these decisions comes with a new ADR in the same PR.
